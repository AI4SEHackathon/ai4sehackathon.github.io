AI for Science and Engineering Hackathon

Thank you for your interest in the AI for Science and Engineering Hackathon.
This is an one-day hackathon for graduate students and researchers exploring AI applications in science and engineering.

  • Learn more

What we do

This one-day hackathon is designed for graduate students and researchers working on computational science, engineering, machine learning, simulation, and related fields. The event aims to provide an opportunity for participants to rapidly prototype ideas, improve ongoing research projects, explore AI tools and workflows, and exchange ideas with other participants.

  • Learn more

Requirments

All participating teams are required to maintain a public project repository (e.g., GitHub or GitLab) as part of the hackathon submission process.
Each team will give a short presentation at the beginning of the event to introduce their project idea and goals, followed by a final presentation/demo at the end of the hackathon to share their progress, results, and future directions.
Because the event capacity is limited, teams will be selected based on project feasibility, relevance to the theme, and readiness to make meaningful progress during the event.
